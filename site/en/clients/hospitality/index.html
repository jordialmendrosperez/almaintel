The situation

Where they started.

Revenue looked different depending on who you asked: the booking system said one thing and the CRM another. Know-how lived in outdated documents and in a few people’s heads, and staff rotated often.

A drop in revenue one month turned out to be rates loaded wrongly, and nobody had noticed for weeks.

What we built

The pieces.

01

Revenue reporting

Occupancy, revenue and promotions by date of stay, traceable down to the booking.

02

Booking and CRM reconciliation

Bookings and CRM deals are compared every morning. Differences become a review list instead of silent corrections.

03

Hourly reviewer

270 checks run every hour and alert the team on Telegram when something looks wrong.

04

Knowledge base

Processes moved from old documents into ClickUp, so new staff can onboard themselves.

A normal day

How it works now.

  1. Every hour, new bookings and payments sync from the booking system.
  2. Early in the morning, bookings are reconciled with the CRM.
  3. The reviewer runs its checks and flags anything unusual on Telegram.
  4. Management opens a dashboard whose numbers match the bookings.
