The situation

Where they started.

Budgets lived in spreadsheets, quotes arrived as PDFs and photos, and crew hours came in as texts at the end of the day. Clients paying on an open-book basis kept asking where their money was going, and the answer took hours to put together.

Finding reliable subcontractors meant days of phone calls, and the owner did not want another tool his team would refuse to open.

What we built

The pieces.

01

Project and bid system

Budget templates, quote comparison by trade, contracts, change orders and payments for every job, in one web app.

02

AI quote reader

Reads a quote from a text, a photo or a PDF and maps it line by line to the budget, so nobody retypes it.

03

Operations assistant

The team logs hours, costs and notes by voice or chat. Nothing is saved until a person confirms it.

04

Subcontractor finder

Uses live county permit data to show which licensed subcontractors are working nearby, and drafts the first message.

A normal day

How it works now.

  1. A foreman sends a voice note with the crew’s hours and the job number.
  2. The assistant matches people, job and cost code, and asks for a quick yes.
  3. The job budget updates. If a line runs over, the project manager gets an alert.
  4. The client opens their link and sees the same numbers the builder sees.
