The situation

Where they started.

The sales team spent days inside a construction-data platform, chasing projects where a competitor’s product had already been specified and arguing to be swapped in late.

The US team also needed help with day-to-day systems, and with knowing where customer data lived.

What we built

The pieces.

01

Lead engine

Reads public agendas in South Florida, finds construction projects before the permit stage and scores them for the product line.

02

Monthly operations support

A set number of hours each month for whatever slows the team down.

03

Information-security review

Where customer data lives, who can reach it, and the policies to protect it.

A normal day

How it works now.

  1. A city publishes its meeting agenda.
  2. The engine extracts each project and the firm designing it.
  3. Every project is scored for fit with the product line.
  4. Sales gets a short list with who to call.
