Step 1 · Checks

What your systems can already confirm.

Each check you automate is one reason an order no longer needs a person. Tick the ones your ERP or price lists could answer today.

Step 2 · Exceptions

Who reviews what.

Every exception goes to one owner, with the reason it failed and the original email or PDF next to the draft.

Step 3 · Rollout

Start with one channel.

  1. 01

    Pick one channel

    The inbox or customers that send you the most orders.

  2. 02

    Run it in the shadow

    For two weeks AI drafts and your team still enters. Compare both.

  3. 03

    Auto-create what passes

    Only orders that pass every check go straight in. The rest go to review.

  4. 04

    Fix one reason a week

    Look at why orders failed and remove the most common cause.
