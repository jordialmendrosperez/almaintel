Would you merge these two contacts?

A match can help you find a record. Deciding what to do with it needs more context.

A new list looks like a simple task: import it and move on. But the CRM already contains some of that information, plus notes and relationships that are absent from the file.

The useful question comes before the import button: what evidence do we need to decide that two records represent the same contact?

Updating does not mean replacing

After identifying the record, each field still needs a rule. An empty phone field can be filled; an existing, different number may need review. A team note needs to retain its provenance.

Relationships matter too. A contact may be linked to a company and an opportunity. Creating a new record that looks cleaner can leave that history disconnected.

Avoiding the duplicate matters. Keeping the context matters too.

Review before applying

  • Separate outcomes: create, update or review.
  • Explain the match: which identifier was used and why.
  • Define the update: which fields are filled and which are not overwritten.
  • Check relationships: notes, companies and opportunities before and after.

Automation can make these rules repeatable. Ambiguous decisions should remain visible to someone who understands the context.
