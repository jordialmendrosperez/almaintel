Before we start

What is useful
to know.

Do we need to replace our tools?

We first review what you already use. If part of it needs to change, we explain why and include it in the scope.

How are price and timing defined?

Around the actual process and its integrations. The proposal defines deliverables, timing, tool costs and support.

What stays with the team?

We agree on permissions, review points and actions requiring confirmation. The system needs to distinguish a proposal from an executed action.

What happens after delivery?

We document how to use it and define support coverage. Ongoing development is agreed around priorities and the capacity needed.
